# Backhaul 0.1.0

First release of Backhaul, a fork of TetherFuseNet
([pyamsoft/tetherfusenet](https://github.com/pyamsoft/tetherfusenet)).

- Share your phone's internet with other devices without root, over Wi-Fi Direct
  or RNDIS, through an HTTP proxy (port 8228) and a SOCKS proxy (port 8229).
- No account, no analytics, no ads, no in-app purchases. Location is required by
  Android to create a Wi-Fi Direct group and is never collected, tracked, or shared.
- See `PRIVACY_POLICY.md` and `TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md` in the repo.
